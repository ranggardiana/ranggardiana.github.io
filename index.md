## Business Research

## Engineering Scalable Multi-Level BOM Architectures: Relational Data Modeling and Dynamic Cost Roll-Up Algorithms for Custom Manufacturing

**Author:** Ardiana Rangga

**Topic:** Enterprise Data Modeling, Production Systems Architecture & Supply Chain Engineering

**Context:** Independent Engineering & Systems Architecture Project (Inspired by Industry Experience in Precision Manufacturing)

## Executive Summary

In high and mix, custom manufacturing environments, such as architectural aluminum fabrication, automotive assemblies, and engineered building envelopes profit margin degradation predominantly originates at the quoting stage. The divergence between estimated costs and actual shop-floor realization stems from three root factors:

1. **Statically coupled, manual Bill of Materials (BOM) breakdowns** unable to resolve parametric variations in real time.
2. **Unaccounted non-linear scrap and yield shrinkage factors** across recursive parent-child assembly tiers.
3. **Latent propagation of fluctuating raw material market indices** and machine work-center operational overheads.

## Self Mini Project

### Solving the "Terserah" Paradoxfor Date-Night Decision Deadlock
Among the most persistent friction points in modern interpersonal relationships is the dinner selection deadlock—colloquially termed in Indonesian dating culture as the **"Terserah" Paradox**.

When Party A asks, *"Mau makan apa?"*, and Party B responds with *"Terserah"* (Whatever), classical logic assumes an open, unconstrained solution space. In practice, however, *"Terserah"* acts as an unvoiced rejection filter:

$$
\forall x \in \text{Suggestions}(A), \quad \text{Probability}(B \text{ accepts } x) \to 0
$$

This paper explores the root psychological mechanics of this deadlock (Decision Paralysis, Risk Externalization, and Asymmetric Vetoes) and presents **TerserahSolver**: a lightweight, web-based bilateral blind swipe consensus engine. By combining anonymous zero-knowledge card mechanics, synchronous state matching, and hard-stop fallback arbitrators, this architecture eliminates relationship friction and delivers dinner resolutions in under 90 seconds.
